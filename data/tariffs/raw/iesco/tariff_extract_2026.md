**2\. NEPRA & Government of Pakistan Uniform Electricity Tariff Schedule (2026)**

Under NEPRA's Calendar Year 2026 determination (Decision dated 07.01.2026 / 12.01.2026, rationalized via Decision No. NEPRA/R/DG(Trf)/TRF-100/2935-57 dated 11.02.2026 and notified via Federal Government SROs 41–52(I)/2026 and SRO 279(I)/2026), the national average determined tariff is Rs. 33.38/kWh. Through a Rs. 248–249 billion federal Tariff Differential Subsidy, the Government of Pakistan enforces a uniform consumer-end tariff across all XWDISCOs and K-Electric.

**CRITICAL 2026 STRUCTURAL RULE: FIXED CHARGES ON DOMESTIC CONSUMERS**

Effective 2026 (SRO 279(I)/2026 & NEPRA Rationalization Decision dated 11.02.2026), monthly fixed charges apply to ALL domestic electricity consumers except Lifeline categories (&lt;=50 units and 51–100 units). For Non-TOU domestic consumers (<5 kW), fixed charges are billed per kW of sanctioned load (Rs. 200 to Rs. 675/kW/month). For TOU domestic consumers (&gt;=5 kW), the Rs. 675/kW/month fixed charge is billed on 50% of sanctioned load or actual MDI, whichever is higher. Where fixed charges apply, separate minimum monthly charges do not apply.

**3\. Dynamic Electricity Adjustments, Surcharges & Federal/Provincial Taxes**

The base energy and fixed charges above constitute roughly 60%–65% of a typical Pakistani electricity bill. The remaining 35%–40% consists of NEPRA-approved dynamic adjustments, statutory surcharges, provincial duties, and federal taxes.
