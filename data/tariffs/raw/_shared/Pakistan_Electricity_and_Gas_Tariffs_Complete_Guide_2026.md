**PAKISTAN ELECTRICITY & NATURAL GAS TARIFF MANUAL**

Complete Regulatory Glossary, NEPRA Electricity Tariff Schedule (All Categories & Slabs), OGRA Natural Gas & LPG Tariffs, Surcharges, Taxes, and Bill Calculation Mechanics (2026)

Regulatory Authorities: NEPRA (Power) & OGRA (Oil & Gas) \| Applicable Utilities: All Ex-WAPDA DISCOs, K-Electric, SNGPL & SSGCL \| Reference Period: 2026

**1\. Comprehensive Glossary of Pakistani Utility Bill & Tariff Terms**

Understanding Pakistani electricity and gas bills requires familiarity with the statutory bodies, utility companies, tariff codes, and dynamic adjustment mechanisms printed on monthly statements.

- **NEPRA (National Electric Power Regulatory Authority):** The federal statutory regulator established under the NEPRA Act 1997. NEPRA determines cost-reflective power generation, transmission, and distribution tariffs, approves monthly Fuel Charges Adjustments (FCA) and Quarterly Tariff Adjustments (QTA), sets consumer service standards, and issues tariff decisions before Federal Government notification in the official Gazette via Statutory Regulatory Orders (SROs).
- **OGRA (Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority):** The federal statutory body established under the OGRA Ordinance 2002. OGRA regulates midstream and downstream oil and gas activities, determines prescribed prices and consumer-end sale tariffs for natural gas utilities (SNGPL and SSGCL), and notifies monthly maximum consumer prices for Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) and Regasified Liquefied Natural Gas (RLNG).
- **DISCO (Electricity Distribution Company):** State-owned regional power distribution utilities formerly part of WAPDA (Ex-WAPDA DISCOs or XWDISCOs): LESCO (Lahore), FESCO (Faisalabad), GEPCO (Gujranwala), IESCO (Islamabad), MEPCO (Multan), PESCO (Peshawar), HESCO (Hyderabad), SEPCO (Sukkur), QESCO (Quetta), and TESCO (Tribal Areas). All XWDISCOs operate under the Federal Government's Uniform Consumer-End Tariff.
- **K-Electric (KE):** A vertically integrated private electric utility serving Karachi and adjoining areas (Dhabeji, Gharo, Hub, Uthal, Vindar, Bela). While NEPRA determines KE's Multi-Year Tariff separately, the Federal Government applies the same Uniform Consumer-End Tariff schedule to KE customers via targeted Tariff Differential Subsidy (TDS).
- **SNGPL & SSGCL:** Pakistan's two state-owned natural gas transmission and distribution utilities. Sui Northern Gas Pipelines Limited (SNGPL) serves Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and Islamabad; Sui Southern Gas Company Limited (SSGCL) serves Sindh and Balochistan. Both apply identical OGRA-notified domestic slab rates.
- **Slab (Consumption Band):** A defined range of monthly energy consumption (measured in kWh/units for electricity, or hm3 / MMBTU for gas) assigned a specific per-unit rate. In Pakistan's electricity tariff, Protected residential consumers receive the telescopic 'one-previous-slab' benefit, whereas Unprotected consumers pay the highest reached slab rate across their entire monthly consumption ('landing-slab' rule).
- **Tariff Category & Code:** The alphanumeric classification code printed on the top section of a utility bill (e.g., A-1a(01) for single-phase domestic &lt;5 kW, A-1b for three-phase &gt;=5 kW, A-2a for commercial, B-1/B-2/B-3/B-4 for industrial, C-1/C-2/C-3 for bulk supply, D-1/D-2 for agricultural tube wells, E-1/E-2 for temporary supply, G for public lighting, H for residential colonies, K for Rawat Lab). It dictates the applicable fixed and variable rate table.
- **Clause (Notification Provision):** A specific numbered paragraph, sub-clause, or annexure condition inside a Gazette SRO or NEPRA/OGRA determination (such as minimum monthly charge clauses, MDI billing rules, or protected-status eligibility rules). Formal billing disputes and legal citations reference the exact SRO number and clause.
- **FPA / FCA (Fuel Price / Fuel Charges Adjustment):** A monthly variable adjustment (Rs./kWh) approved by NEPRA to pass through the difference between the actual monthly fuel cost of power generation (RLNG, coal, furnace oil, hydel, nuclear, renewables) and the reference fuel cost built into the base tariff. Because hearings occur after month-end, FCA typically appears on consumer bills with a 1-to-2-month lag and can be positive (surcharge) or negative (credit).
- **QTA (Quarterly Tariff Adjustment):** A per-unit adjustment (Rs./kWh) determined by NEPRA every quarter to reconcile variations in capacity charges, transmission and distribution losses, variable O&M, and impact of incremental sales across DISCOs. QTA remains uniform for three consecutive months.
- **Sanctioned Load & MDI (Maximum Demand Indicator):** Sanctioned Load is the maximum electrical capacity (in kW) approved and registered for a connection in the utility's contract. Maximum Demand Indicator (MDI) records the highest average kW load drawn during any 30-minute interval in a billing month. For connections >=5 kW (and commercial/industrial/bulk tariffs), monthly fixed charges are billed on actual MDI or a specified percentage (25% or 50%) of sanctioned load, whichever is higher.
- **Time of Use (TOU) Metering:** A dual-register tariff system mandatory for connections with sanctioned load of 5 kW and above (and optional for <5 kW). It bills electricity consumed during peak evening hours (typically 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM or 7:00 PM – 11:00 PM depending on season) at a significantly higher Peak rate, and all remaining 20 hours at a lower Off-Peak rate.
- **Protected vs. Unprotected Consumer:** A classification governing domestic electricity and gas subsidies. For electricity, a residential consumer (<5 kW) is 'Protected' only if monthly consumption stays at or below 200 units for 6 consecutive months; exceeding 200 units even once strips Protected status for the next 6 months. For natural gas, 'Protected' status applies to domestic households whose average winter consumption (Nov–Feb) remains at or below 0.9 hm3 (90 cubic meters).
- **Estimated / Average Reading:** Billing issued without an actual physical meter reading (often marked as 'DEF' for defective meter, 'LOK' for locked premises, or 'EST' for estimated). Utilities estimate units based on historical consumption averages—a frequent source of overbilling and artificial slab jumping until corrected via actual meter verification.
- **hm3 and MMBTU (Gas Billing Units):** Gas meters record volume in cubic meters (m3). Bills convert m3 into hecto-cubic meters (1 hm3 = 100 m3) to determine the consumption slab, and then convert volume into thermal energy measured in Million British Thermal Units (MMBTU) using the local gas field's Gross Calorific Value (GCV) and pressure factor. The OGRA rate (Rs./MMBTU) is multiplied by consumed MMBTU.

**2\. NEPRA & Government of Pakistan Uniform Electricity Tariff Schedule (2026)**

Under NEPRA's Calendar Year 2026 determination (Decision dated 07.01.2026 / 12.01.2026, rationalized via Decision No. NEPRA/R/DG(Trf)/TRF-100/2935-57 dated 11.02.2026 and notified via Federal Government SROs 41–52(I)/2026 and SRO 279(I)/2026), the national average determined tariff is Rs. 33.38/kWh. Through a Rs. 248–249 billion federal Tariff Differential Subsidy, the Government of Pakistan enforces a uniform consumer-end tariff across all XWDISCOs and K-Electric.

**CRITICAL 2026 STRUCTURAL RULE: FIXED CHARGES ON DOMESTIC CONSUMERS**

Effective 2026 (SRO 279(I)/2026 & NEPRA Rationalization Decision dated 11.02.2026), monthly fixed charges apply to ALL domestic electricity consumers except Lifeline categories (&lt;=50 units and 51–100 units). For Non-TOU domestic consumers (<5 kW), fixed charges are billed per kW of sanctioned load (Rs. 200 to Rs. 675/kW/month). For TOU domestic consumers (&gt;=5 kW), the Rs. 675/kW/month fixed charge is billed on 50% of sanctioned load or actual MDI, whichever is higher. Where fixed charges apply, separate minimum monthly charges do not apply.

**4\. OGRA Natural Gas, RLNG & LPG Tariff Schedule (SNGPL & SSGCL — 2026)**

Natural gas consumer tariffs are regulated by OGRA and notified by the Ministry of Energy (Petroleum Division). While SNGPL and SSGCL petitioned OGRA in November 2026 for revised FY 2026-27 prescribed prices (Rs. 1,712.40/MMBTU for SNGPL and Rs. 2,071.75/MMBTU for SSGCL), consumer-end sale tariffs remain governed by the notified OGRA schedule effective July 1, 2025 (confirmed unchanged through FY 2025-26/2026 determinations) until a new Federal Government notification is issued.

**HOW YOUR GAS BILL CONVERTS METER UNITS (m3) TO RUPEES (MMBTU)**

1\. Your gas meter measures volume in cubic meters (m3). Divide m3 by 100 to get hecto-cubic meters (hm3). For example, 150 m3 = 1.50 hm3.  
2\. Your hm3 volume determines which slab rate applies (e.g., 1.50 hm3 falls in the 'Up to 1.5 hm3' slab).  
3\. SNGPL/SSGCL multiplies your consumed hm3 by a Pressure Factor and the local gas field's Gross Calorific Value (GCV, typically 950–1,050 BTU/SCF) divided by 281.7385 to compute exact thermal energy in MMBTU.  
4\. Gas Charges = Consumed MMBTU x Slab Rate (Rs./MMBTU). Then add Monthly Fixed Charge + Meter Rent (Rs. 40), and apply 18% GST on the combined subtotal.

**5.1 Worked Electricity Bill Example: Protected (195 Units) vs. Unprotected (205 Units)**

| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1\. Variable Energy Charges**           | (100 x Rs. 10.54) + (95 x Rs. 13.01) = Rs. 2,289.45   | 205 x Rs. 33.10 (Landing Slab 201–300) = Rs. 6,785.50   |
| **2\. Monthly Fixed Charges**             | 2 kW x Rs. 300/kW = Rs. 600.00                        | 2 kW x Rs. 350/kW = Rs. 700.00                          |

| **4\. FC Surcharge + NJ Surcharge**       | (195 x Rs. 0.43) + (195 x Rs. 0.10) = Rs. 103.35      | (205 x Rs. 3.23) + (205 x Rs. 0.10) = Rs. 682.65        |
| **5\. Electricity Duty (1.5% of Energy)** | 1.5% x Rs. 2,289.45 = Rs. 34.34                       | 1.5% x Rs. 6,785.50 = Rs. 101.78                        |
| **6\. General Sales Tax (18% GST)**       | 18% x (2,289.45 + 600 + 292.50 + 103.35) = Rs. 591.35 | 18% x (6,785.50 + 700 + 307.50 + 682.65) = Rs. 1,525.62 |
